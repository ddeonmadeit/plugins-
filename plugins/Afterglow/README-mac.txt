AFTERGLOW: PLAYING IT
  The big window is a sunset. Drag the sun: left is still, right is moving (vibrato, phaser, tape
  wobble, drift); high is bright and open, low and sinking is dark. Double-click the sun to put
  the preset back as designed.
  The six knobs underneath bend the whole preset: SWELL (attack), TAIL (release), BLEND (layer A
  vs B), GRIT (saturation and lo-fi), ECHO (delay) and SPACE (reverb). Centre = as designed.
  The lamps switch effects on and off: tape, ensemble, phaser, delay, reverb and pump.
  Pads like held chords and the sustain pedal; leads (mono / legato presets) like overlapping notes.
