Hush: a de-esser with a level for every sound

1. Put Hush on the vocal track (FL Studio: Mixer > vocal track; Ableton: on the vocal track, and work
   in Arrangement View: Session clips have no fixed place in the song, so a take can't line up).
2. TRANSFER is lit: press play over the part you want to de-ess, then stop. Every S, SH and T appears.
3. Drag a sound's handle in the lower lane down to turn just that sound down. Amount sets all the others.
   Click a sound to hear it (your keys then go to Hush: Cmd+Z undoes); Listen plays only what is removed.
4. BOUNCE renders the result to Music/ddeonmadeit/Hush/Bounces; drag DRAG OUT into the playlist at
   bar 1 (Ableton: onto an audio track at 1.1.1, then switch the clip's Warp off).

Takes are kept in Music/ddeonmadeit/Hush/Takes and found again when you reopen the project.
