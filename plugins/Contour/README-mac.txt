Contour: note-by-note vocal editing

1. Put Contour first on the vocal track (FL Studio: Mixer > vocal track > slot 1; Ableton: the first
   device on the vocal track, and work in Arrangement View: Session clips have no fixed place in the
   song, so a take can't line up with them).
2. Play the song: Contour picks up the vocal by itself (FOLLOW is lit in the top bar) and the notes
   appear. Your edits play live in the mix; nothing to record or drag out. Change or re-record a part
   and it picks the new take up as it plays.
3. Drag notes to change pitch or timing; use the tools above the notes for vibrato, drift, formant,
   level and split. Click the notes first so your keys go to Contour: Cmd+Z undoes, Space plays from
   the cursor when the song is stopped (while it plays, Space still stops your DAW).
4. Optional: PRINT renders the edit to Music/ddeonmadeit/Contour/Bounces; drag DRAG OUT into the playlist at
   bar 1 (Ableton: onto an audio track at 1.1.1, then switch the clip's Warp off).

Takes are kept in Music/ddeonmadeit/Contour/Takes and found again when you reopen the project.
