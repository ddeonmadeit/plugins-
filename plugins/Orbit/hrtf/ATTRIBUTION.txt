Resources/hrtf.bin is derived from:

  The Viking HRTF dataset v2
  Simone Spagnol, Riccardo Miccini and Runar Unnthorsson (2020)
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4160401
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Changes: four of the 21 pinna sets (subjects L, G, A, E), equalised against a blend of the diffuse field
and the frontal response, spectrally smoothed, mirrored left/right, rebuilt as 64-tap minimum-phase filters
on a uniform 5 degree grid, quantised to 16 bits. Built by make_hrtf.py in this folder.
