Free VST3 + AU instruments and effects for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), for FL Studio and Ableton Live (and any DAW that loads VST3 or AU). Every download link on this page always points at the newest release.

Install without the macOS warning

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste this line and press Return. It installs every plugin, or updates the ones you already have, and macOS won't say “Apple could not verify…”. When it asks for your Mac password, type it and press Return (nothing shows while you type).
